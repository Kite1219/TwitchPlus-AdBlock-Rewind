# TwitchPlus v1.5

Promoted from v1.5 Beta 5 without changing the IPA.

- Visible side chat reserves video space; closing chat expands it with aspect-fit playback.
- Native controls and the rewind timeline follow the final video bounds, including portrait height changes.
- Native layout constraints and tap recognizers are restored/refreshed around rotation without replacing their targets or delegates.
- Compact Duo navigation, saved settings, optional rewind and the native playlist filter are retained.

Use the sliders button on the timeline to open Rewind Settings. Its installed heading still identifies the original Beta 5 build.

Built from commit `179e0c90f795c70523b4407358a42989f2b3af44`. Recorded checks: 66 host tests, 14 compiled-core tests, 7 packaging tests, 30 playlist tests, 3 preservation tests and 10 UIKit-fixture checks.

The fixtures do not run Twitch or verify physical-device playback, rotation or actual ad breaks. This release makes no new ad-block effectiveness claim. Only the two replacement libraries differ from v1.4; the main executable and Info.plist are preserved.
