# Changelog

## 1.2.2 Beta 6

Performance update. Removes the duplicate startup seek, combines rapid skips, preserves ready preparation during refresh, rejects stale/insufficient prepared sources, prepares earlier, and retries temporary preparation failures sooner. UI and video quality are unchanged. 35 host tests passed; no device speedup has been measured yet.

## 1.2.1 Beta 5

Red/dark LIVE badge above the right end of the full-width purple timeline. Timestamp hidden at live and shown while dragging or rewound. Circular skip controls grouped closer to the center. White thumb only while dragging.

## 1.2.0 Beta 4

Elapsed HH:MM:SS timestamps and live drag preview. Circular skip icons and a minimal drag-only thumb.

## Earlier builds

Native read-only chat fallback, local Forced Follow with an iPad sidebar fix, native rewind, ten-second skips, and a return-to-live shortcut. Existing TwitchAdBlock functionality is preserved from the supplied base.
